## SSC MTS & Havaldar 2026 Overview

The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** has released the official recruitment notice for **Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff (MTS)** and **Havaldar in Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN)**. With **9,583 vacancies**, this is one of India's largest central government opportunities for candidates who have completed 10th grade (Matriculation).

## Eligibility Criteria

* **Educational Qualification:** Candidates must have passed the Matriculation (10th Class) Examination or equivalent from a recognized Board.
* **Age Limit:**
  * For MTS: 18 to 25 Years
  * For Havaldar (CBIC/CBN) and few MTS cadres: 18 to 27 Years
  * Relaxations: OBC: 3 Years, SC/ST: 5 Years, PwD: 10 Years.

---

## Examination Pattern

The Computer-Based Examination (CBE) will be conducted in **Hindi, English, and 13 regional languages** (including Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Konkani, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu):

* **Session-I:** Numerical and Mathematical Ability (20 Questions, 60 Marks) & Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving (20 Questions, 60 Marks). No negative marking in Session-I.
* **Session-II:** General Awareness (25 Questions, 75 Marks) & English Language and Comprehension (25 Questions, 75 Marks). Negative marking of 1 mark for each wrong answer.
* **Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST):** Only for Havaldar posts (Walking: 1600 meters in 15 minutes for males; 1 km in 20 minutes for females).

## Application Fee

* **General / OBC / EWS Men:** ₹100/-
* **Women, SC, ST, PwD, Ex-Servicemen:** **Free (Exempted)**

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).
2. Register on the new SSC portal and obtain your login ID.
3. Select **MTS & Havaldar Examination 2026**.
4. Fill your state preferences for posting.
5. Capture real-time live photo and upload signature.
6. Submit application and print the confirmation page.
