## UPSC Civil Services 2026 Recruitment Notice

The **Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** has announced the prestigious **Civil Services Examination (CSE 2026)** and **Indian Forest Service (IFS 2026)**. Eligible Indian citizens seeking recruitment to the **Indian Administrative Service (IAS)**, **Indian Police Service (IPS)**, **Indian Foreign Service (IFS)**, **Indian Revenue Service (IRS)**, and other premier Group 'A' and Group 'B' administrative services must apply through the One-Time Registration (OTR) platform.

## Eligibility Criteria

* **Nationality:** Candidate must be a citizen of India for IAS, IFS, and IPS.
* **Educational Qualification:** Candidate must hold a Graduate Degree in any discipline from any recognized University or institution incorporated by an Act of the Central or State Legislature. Candidates appearing in their final year degree examinations are also eligible to apply for Preliminary exam.
* **Age Limits (as on prescribed cut-off date):**
  * Minimum Age: 21 Years
  * Maximum Age: 32 Years
  * Relaxations: OBC (3 years / 9 attempts), SC/ST (5 years / unlimited attempts), PwD (10 years).

---

## Scheme of Selection

1. **Preliminary Examination:** Two objective type papers (General Studies Paper-I and CSAT Paper-II, 200 marks each). CSAT is qualifying with 33% marks.
2. **Main Examination (Written):** 9 subjective descriptive papers assessing comprehensive analytical writing skills.
3. **Personality Test (Interview):** Board interview carrying 275 marks to evaluate mental alertness, integrity, and leadership qualities.

---

## How to Submit Application

1. Go to the UPSC official portal: [https://upsconline.nic.in](https://upsconline.nic.in).
2. Register in the **One Time Registration (OTR)** module if not already registered.
3. Access the active notifications tab and select **Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026**.
4. Choose exam centers for Preliminary and Mains examinations.
5. Upload photo identity proof and recent portrait photo.
6. Submit application fee (₹100 for Gen/OBC males; Female/SC/ST/PwD are exempt).
7. Download and retain the application receipt.
