## Indian Army Agniveer 2026 Overview

The **Indian Army (Ministry of Defence)** invites online applications from motivated young Indian citizens (male and female) for recruitment under the **Agnipath Scheme 2026**. Recruitment rallies will be conducted across all Zonal Recruiting Offices (ZROs) and Army Recruiting Offices (AROs) for General Duty (GD), Technical, Store Keeper / Clerk, and Tradesmen categories.

| Particulars | Information |

## Category-Wise Educational Criteria

* **Agniveer (General Duty) (All Arms):** Class 10th / Matric pass with 45% marks in aggregate and minimum 33% in each subject.
* **Agniveer (Technical) (All Arms):** 10+2 / Intermediate exam pass in Science with Physics, Chemistry, Maths and English with 50% marks in aggregate and 40% in each subject.
* **Agniveer (Clerk / Store Keeper Technical):** 10+2 / Intermediate pass in any stream (Arts, Commerce, Science) with 60% marks in aggregate and minimum 50% in English and Maths/Accounts/Book Keeping.
* **Agniveer (Tradesmen):** Class 10th simple pass or Class 8th simple pass with 33% in each subject.

## Physical Fitness Standards (PFT)

1. **1.6 Km Run:**
   * Group I: Up to 5 Min 30 Sec (60 Marks)
   * Group II: 5 Min 31 Sec to 5 Min 45 Sec (48 Marks)
2. **Beam (Pull Ups):** 10 pull ups (40 marks) down to 6 pull ups (16 marks).
3. **9 Feet Ditch Jump:** Need to qualify.
4. **Zig-Zag Balance:** Need to qualify.

---

## Selection Stages

1. **Phase-I:** Online Common Entrance Exam (CEE).
2. **Phase-II:** Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Physical Measurement Test (PMT), and Adaptability Test at rally venue.
3. **Phase-III:** Comprehensive Medical Examination.

## Step-by-Step Online Registration

1. Visit the official website: [https://joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in).
2. Click on **Agnipath** -> **Apply Online / Registration**.
3. Complete Aadhaar / Matriculation certificate authentication.
4. Fill personal, contact, and educational details.
5. Upload clear passport photo with light background and signature.
6. Submit application and download the registration slip.
