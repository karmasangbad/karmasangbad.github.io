## SSC CGL 2026 Recruitment Overview

The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** has officially released the recruitment advertisement for the **Combined Graduate Level Examination (CGL 2026)**. This national-level recruitment drive offers over **15,000+ vacancies** across premier Central Government Ministries, Constitutional Bodies, and Attached Departments, including Income Tax, Central Excise (Inspector), Assistant Section Officer (CSS, MEA, IB), Sub-Inspector (CBI, NIA), and Divisional Accountant.

## Post-Wise Eligibility Criteria

* **Educational Qualification:** Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or Institute established by an Act of Parliament.
* **Age Limit:** Generally between **18 to 30/32 years** depending on the specific cadre.
* **Age Relaxation:** 
  * OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): 3 years
  * SC / ST: 5 years
  * PwD: 10 to 15 years
  * Ex-Servicemen: As per Central Govt guidelines.

---

## Selection Process Scheme

1. **Tier-I Examination:** Computer-Based Objective Test (Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension).
2. **Tier-II Examination:** Computer-Based Test (Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Module, and Data Entry Speed Test).
3. **Document Verification & Medical Fitness:** Conducted by user departments upon selection.

---

## Application Fee Details

* **General / OBC / EWS Male Candidates:** ₹100/-
* **Women Candidates, SC, ST, PwD, and ESM:** **NIL (Exempted)**
* Payment mode: BHIM UPI, Net Banking, Visa, MasterCard, RuPay Debit Card.

## How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

1. Navigate to the official SSC one-stop portal: [https://ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in).
2. Complete your **One-Time Registration (OTR)** if you are a first-time user.
3. Log in with your Registration Number and Password.
4. Click on **Apply** under **Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026**.
5. Fill in exam center choices, educational qualifications, and correspondence details.
6. Capture your live photograph using the official webcam/mobile interface and upload signature.
7. Pay the application fee online (if applicable) and preview the submitted form.
8. Save and print the confirmation page for your records.
