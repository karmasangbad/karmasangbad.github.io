## Bank PO 2026 Recruitment Overview

The **Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)** and the **State Bank of India (SBI)** have published recruitment notices for over **6,000+ Probationary Officer (PO) & Management Trainee** positions in 11 participating public sector banks including Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank, Union Bank of India, Indian Bank, and SBI branches nationwide.

## Educational Eligibility & Age

* **Qualification:** A Graduation Degree in any discipline (B.A, B.Sc, B.Com, B.E, B.Tech, BBA, BCA, etc.) from a University recognized by the Government of India.
* **Computer Literacy:** Working knowledge of computer operations is required.
* **Age Limit:** 20 to 30 Years as on prescribed cutoff date. Standard relaxations apply for reserved categories (SC/ST: 5 years, OBC: 3 years, PwD: 10 years).

---

## Examination Structure

1. **Preliminary Exam:** Objective online test covering English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability (100 Marks, 60 Minutes).
2. **Main Exam:** Objective + Descriptive test (Data Analysis & Interpretation, General & Banking Awareness, Reasoning, English, and Essay/Letter Writing).
3. **Interview & Group Discussion:** Psychometric evaluation, Group Exercise, and Interview round carrying 100 marks.

## How to Apply Online

1. Visit the official recruitment portal: [https://www.ibps.in](https://www.ibps.in).
2. Click on the direct link for **Apply Online for CRP PO/MT**.
3. Complete the registration and receive your Provisional Registration ID and Password.
4. Fill in academic details, category, and preferred bank preferences.
5. Upload passport photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and hand-written declaration.
6. Pay application fee through online payment gateway and print receipt.
