## RRB ALP 2026 Recruitment Overview

The **Ministry of Railways (Railway Recruitment Control Board)** has issued the Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) for recruitment of **18,799 Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) and Technician vacancies** across 21 Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) including RRB Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, Allahabad (Prayagraj), Patna, Ranchi, Secunderabad, and Chandigarh.

### Fast Facts & Overview

| Key Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruiting Authority** | Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) |
| **Notice Reference** | CEN 01/2026 (ALP & Technician) |
| **Total Vacancies** | 18,799 Posts |
| **Pay Scale** | Level-2 of 7th CPC Pay Matrix (Initial Pay ₹19,900/- + Allowances) |
| **Job Location** | Across 17 Zonal Railways in India |
| **Application Mode** | Online via central portal |
| **Official Website** | [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in) |

---

## Minimum Educational Eligibility

1. **Matriculation (10th Pass)** from a recognized Board, **AND**
2. **ITI / NCVT / SCVT Trade Certificate** in Fitter, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, Millwright, Maintenance Mechanic, Mechanic Radio & TV, Electronics Mechanic, Mechanic Motor Vehicle, Wireman, Tractor Mechanic, Armature & Coil Winder, etc., **OR**
3. **3-Year Diploma** in Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile Engineering, **OR**
4. **B.E / B.Tech Degree** in engineering branches recognized by AICTE.

---

## Stages of Examination

* **CBT-1:** First Stage Computer-Based Test (Screening, 75 Questions, 60 Minutes).
* **CBT-2 (Part A & Part B):** Second Stage CBT for merit evaluation and technical trade competence.
* **CBAT:** Computer-Based Aptitude Test (Psychological test for ALP post).
* **Document Verification & Medical Examination:** Strict A-1 Medical Standard (Distant Vision 6/6, 6/6 without glasses).

---

## Application Fee

* **All Candidates (General / OBC):** ₹500/- (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1).
* **SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, Female, Transgender, Minorities, EBC:** ₹250/- (Full ₹250 refunded after appearing in CBT-1).

## How to Apply on RRB Portal

1. Visit the central railway portal: [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in).
2. Create an account with your mobile number and valid email address.
3. Select your chosen Railway Zone / RRB carefully.
4. Fill personal, educational, and trade qualifications accurately.
5. Upload recent passport photograph and signature as per prescribed pixel dimensions.
6. Submit fee and download application confirmation.
