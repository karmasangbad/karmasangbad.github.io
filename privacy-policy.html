Privacy Policy

Welcome to KarmaSangbad (karmasangbad.github.io). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and job aspirants across India.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar, PAN, passwords, or banking information. Standard non-personal technical data (such as browser type, operating system, and IP address) may be logged automatically for analytics and website performance monitoring.

2. External & Third-Party Links

KarmaSangbad provides direct hyperlinks to official government recruitment portals (e.g. UPSC, SSC, RRB, IBPS, State PSCs). When you navigate to external domains, their respective privacy policies and terms of service will apply.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies or web beacons to understand traffic patterns and enhance candidate user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your personal browser settings at any time.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or queries regarding this privacy statement, please contact us at contact@karmasangbad.com.
